## Open Data Portals

Government databases, statistics, court records, and public data repositories.

### Federal Data
- **[Data.gov](https://data.gov)** - Federal government's primary open data site with 368,000+ datasets covering all government agencies
- **[US Census Bureau](https://data.census.gov)** - Official Census data platform with demographic, economic, and population statistics
- **[NASA Open Data](https://data.nasa.gov)** - NASA's metadata repository for space exploration, science, and aeronautics datasets
- **[USPTO Data Portal](https://data.uspto.gov)** - Patent and trademark data from the US Patent and Trademark Office
- **[GSA Open Data](https://open.gsa.gov/data/)** - General Services Administration datasets and APIs

### Courts
- **[PACER Federal Courts](https://pacer.uscourts.gov)** - Public Access to Court Electronic Records with 1+ billion federal court documents
- **[RECAP Archive](https://free.law/recap)** - Free database of PACER documents and court opinions (no account required)
- **[CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com)** - Free searchable database of court opinions and federal case law
- **[PACER Case Locator](https://pcl.uscourts.gov)** - National index for district, bankruptcy, and appellate court cases
- **[BKData Bankruptcy Search](https://www.bkdata.com)** - Bankruptcy case search and document retrieval

### Stock & Financial Markets
- **[SEC EDGAR Database](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/)** - Securities and Exchange Commission corporate filings database
- **[FINRA BrokerCheck](https://brokercheck.finra.org)** - Financial Industry Regulatory Authority broker and investment advisor records
- **[NYSE Market Data](https://www.nyse.com/market-data/real-time)** - New York Stock Exchange trading data and statistics
- **[NASDAQ Market Data](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-data)** - NASDAQ exchange trading information and market statistics

### Government Transparency
- **[MuckRock](https://www.muckrock.com/)** - File and browse Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests
- **[GovernmentAttic](https://www.governmentattic.org/)** - FOIA documents from federal agencies
- **[OpenPoliceData](https://openpolicedata.com/)** - Database of 425+ US datasets on police use of force, traffic stops, and officer-involved incidents

## Vehicles

Vehicle registration, identification, and tracking resources divided by category.

### Land Vehicles
- **[NHTSA VIN Decoder](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/decoder/)** - Official National Highway Traffic Safety Administration VIN decoder
- **[NICB VINCheck](https://www.nicb.org/vincheck)** - National Insurance Crime Bureau stolen vehicle database (free, 5 searches per day)
- **[National Motor Vehicle Title System](https://vehiclehistory.bja.ojp.gov/)** - Federal system designed to protect consumers from fraud and unsafe vehicles

### Maritime Vessels
- **[Marine Traffic](https://www.marinetraffic.com)** - Global ship tracking with vessel identification and movement data
- **[Vessel Finder](https://www.vesselfinder.com)** - Real-time ship tracking and maritime vessel information
- **[Coast Guard Vessel Search](https://cgmix.uscg.mil/psix/psixsearch.aspx)** - US Coast Guard documented vessel search database
- **[Naval Vessel Register](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Resources/Naval-Vessel-Register/)** - US Navy ship inventory and vessel information database

### Aviation
- **[FAA Aircraft Registry](https://registry.faa.gov/aircraftinquiry/)** - Federal Aviation Administration aircraft registration database
- **[FAA Releasable Aircraft Database](https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry/releasable_aircraft_download)** - Complete downloadable FAA aircraft registration database
- **[FlightAware](https://flightaware.com)** - Flight tracking with aircraft registration and ownership data

## 🔍 Search Tips & Best Practices

### Link Validation Status
- ✅ **Validated September 2025**: All links tested and confirmed working
- ⚠️ **Note**: Some government websites experienced temporary outages during validation

### OSINT Investigation Guidelines
1. **Start with Free Resources**: Always begin with free, publicly accessible databases
2. **Cross-Reference Information**: Verify findings across multiple sources
3. **Document Sources**: Keep detailed records of all data sources and collection dates
4. **Legal Compliance**: Ensure all research complies with applicable laws and regulations
5. **Privacy Considerations**: Respect privacy rights and use information responsibly

### Recent Changes (2025)
- **Corporate Transparency Act**: New beneficial ownership reporting requirements for US entities
- **Enhanced Security**: Some government databases have implemented additional security measures
- **Data Access**: Ongoing changes to government data accessibility policies
